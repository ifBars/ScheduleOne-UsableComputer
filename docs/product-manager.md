# Product Manager

Products shows the game's discovered products with their native artwork. Search matches names and product types without regard to case. Click the state filter to cycle through all, listed, unlisted, and favourite products. The type control cycles through discovered types and back to all types.

The sort control cycles through name, highest value, and lowest value. Prices sort numerically; equal prices use name and product ID for stable ordering. Search and both filters work together. An empty result explains how to get back to your catalogue.

Compact mode shows more products; large tiles enlarge the images and text. Columns adapt to the window width. Use the scrollbar or mouse wheel to reach later rows. The highlighted tile identifies the product in the preview, where you can change its listing or favourite state through the game's product service. Action feedback stays visible briefly while native data refreshes.

Search, filters, density, and selection belong to the open app session. Product discovery, listing, and favourites remain owned by the game. Unavailable product data is distinguished from an empty catalogue, and unavailable actions are disabled.
